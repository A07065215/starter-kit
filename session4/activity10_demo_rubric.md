# Demo rehearsal rubric (Activity 10)

Present a 3-minute demo to a partner using: **Problem, Insight, Next step**.
Partner scores 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent), then writes the single most useful fix.

| Criterion | What good looks like | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | The client's decision is stated in the first 30 seconds | /5 |
| Insight | One scenario walked end to end: data, prediction, decision | /5 |
| Trust | Limits, assumptions and uncertainty are said out loud | /5 |
| Polish | Nothing breaks; loading and error states look intentional | /5 |

**Top fix (one sentence):** ______________________________________________

Quick usability check: system status visible? errors recoverable? filters labeled? every chart answers one question?
